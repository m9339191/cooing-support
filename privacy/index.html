Cooing · Privacy policy

Your records.
Your device. Your choice.

How Cooing for iOS handles your information.

Device-local information

Child profiles, dates of birth, care records, photos, vaccinations, recorder names, separate parent health records and settings are stored on your device. The developer does not transmit these records to a developer server. The app has no developer account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

Permissions

Photos, camera and notification permissions are requested when you use the relevant feature. Denying a permission does not disable unrelated journaling. Reminder text may appear on your lock screen according to device settings.

Backups and sharing

Exports and sharing occur only at your request, through the destination you select. ZIP backups include all children, photos and separate parent health records and are not encrypted. Choose a trusted location and recipient. The app does not provide automatic cloud synchronization or live collaboration. Other apps, personal cloud services and OS backups follow their own policies.

Apple services

Payments and restoration use Apple StoreKit; the developer does not receive payment-card information. The app checks Apple-verified transactions. Trial timing state is stored in device Keychain and may persist after uninstall. Update checks and reviews use official Apple services. Apple handles transaction, network and diagnostic data under its policies.

Deletion and retention

Records can be deleted in the app. A pre-restore journal checkpoint is retained locally for recovery. Removed photo files may remain in app storage. To remove local app data, use Delete App in iOS settings; Offload App preserves data. External files and cloud/device backups must be managed separately. The developer cannot access or erase your local journal remotely.

Contact and voluntary information

Support information is received only when you choose to send it. Do not send backups containing child names, health records or photos. During TestFlight beta testing, Apple sends the developer beta crash logs, usage information and tester details such as name and email under its TestFlight terms. Feedback you submit is also shared. This is separate from the device-local care journal. This website’s hosting service may process technical information needed to deliver pages under its policies. Contact details appear below.
