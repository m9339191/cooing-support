Getting started, purchases, photos, reminders and safe backups.

Getting started

Choose an app language, enter the child’s name and birth date, then select the country for health guidance. Growth and vaccination use that country’s references; each can be changed in the child profile. App language is independent of country.

Trial and lifetime purchase

After the 7-day device trial, a one-time purchase enables continued entry and editing. There is no subscription or automatic charge. Reading, export, deletion and backup restoration remain available. Use Settings → Lifetime access → Restore purchases with the same Apple account and an internet connection. Pricing and refunds are handled by Apple.

Photos and reminders

Choose Add photo in a record editor. Check Cooing permissions in iOS Settings if access was denied. Reminders are local notifications. Delivery presentation and sound depend on notification permissions, Focus, mute and volume settings.

Backup and restore

In Settings → Backup and restore, export a ZIP and save it to Files. Verify that a file was actually saved. Select that ZIP to restore, check the preview and confirm. Restore replaces the journal with the selected snapshot; a pre-restore copy is retained locally. Backups contain sensitive health records and photos and are not encrypted.

Growth, vaccination and country references

Routine vaccination references are available for South Korea, the United States, Japan, China and Vietnam. Growth uses Korea’s 2017 charts (WHO below age 3), WHO below 24 months then CDC in the US, Japan’s 2023 survey, China’s WS/T 423–2022 and WHO in Vietnam. Screens show sources and supported ages. Japanese and Chinese percentiles interpolate published tables; values outside the published band show ≤3 or ≥97. The app does not diagnose, prescribe vaccinations or assess catch-up schedules. Confirm actual vaccination and growth interpretation with clinicians and official health authorities.

Contact support

Include the app version, device model, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Hide child names and health information in screenshots. Never send private journals, photos, complete backups or Apple account passwords. Contact details appear below.
